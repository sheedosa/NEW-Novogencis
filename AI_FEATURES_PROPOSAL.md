# Novogenics — AI Features Proposal

A new direction for how artificial intelligence can support the clinic — making it easier for new patients to find us, saving the team hours every week, and keeping existing patients engaged between treatments.

The features below are grouped by where they would live: the public website, the daily running of the clinic, and the patient portal.

---

## On the Website — Helping New Patients Find Us

### 1. Hair Loss Photo Analyser

A visitor uploads or takes a photo of their scalp. Within seconds they receive a personalised hair-loss profile showing the stage of their hair loss, the pattern, and an early indication of whether they're likely to be a strong candidate for treatment.

**Why it helps:**
- Turns curious browsers into qualified, named leads — they need to enter their email to receive their full report.
- Most competitor clinics now offer this. Novogenics offering a smarter version positions us as the modern choice.
- Patients arrive at the first consultation already informed, so the consultation itself is faster and more focused.

---

### 2. 24/7 AI Concierge

A chat window on every page of the website that can answer questions about treatments, prices, the team, the clinic, and the booking process. It can also book in consultations directly, without a human needing to be available.

**Why it helps:**
- The majority of enquiries that come through right now are repeat questions about pricing, suitability, and the difference between treatments. This handles them automatically.
- Captures leads at 11pm, on weekends, on bank holidays — times when no one is at the clinic to answer the phone.
- Hands clinical questions over to Dr Aminah's inbox so nothing important falls through the cracks.

---

### 3. Smart Lead Prioritising

Every new assessment that comes through the website is quietly assessed in the background and given a priority score based on how likely the person is to actually book and pay for a treatment.

**Why it helps:**
- The team sees the most promising enquiries at the top of the list with a clear flag.
- Doctors spend less time chasing dead leads and more time converting the warm ones.
- Conversion rates improve without anyone having to work harder.

---

### 4. Personalised Treatment Recommendation

A short, friendly interactive quiz that visitors can take. At the end they receive a personalised PDF recommending the treatment path they're best suited for, based on their answers.

**Why it helps:**
- A softer entry point than the full assessment for visitors who aren't quite ready to commit.
- The PDF carries the Novogenics name and stays with them long after they leave the site.
- Excellent content to share on social media and in email campaigns.

---

### 5. AI-Assisted Blog Writing

When the team wants to publish a blog post on a topic — say, hair loss in women after pregnancy — they describe it in one sentence and receive a well-written, on-brand draft to review and publish.

**Why it helps:**
- Regular content on the website helps the clinic appear higher in Google search results — meaning more organic visitors for free.
- A blog post that would normally take an hour to write is ready to review in two minutes.
- Builds the clinic's authority and trust over time.

---

## In the Clinic — Saving the Team Time

### 6. Voice-to-Notes

After a treatment session, the doctor taps a button and speaks freely for a minute or two about what happened — what they saw, what they did, what's next. The system writes up structured clinical notes automatically and files them in the patient's record.

**Why it helps:**
- Saves each doctor close to an hour every working day — time that can be spent on more patients or going home earlier.
- Clinical notes are more consistent and more complete because nothing gets forgotten in the rush between appointments.
- Reduces evening admin work to almost zero.

---

### 7. Patient Progress Photo Comparison

The doctor selects two photos from a patient's record — for example a "before" and a recent progress shot. The system describes the changes in plain language and highlights where improvement is visible.

**Why it helps:**
- Gives the patient a clear, written summary of their progress that they can read at home and share with family.
- Helps the doctor explain results during the consultation without having to study both photos in detail.
- Creates beautiful "before and after" content for marketing (with patient permission).

---

### 8. No-Show Prediction

The system looks at every upcoming appointment and quietly flags the ones most likely to be a no-show. Those patients get an extra reminder the day before, by text or WhatsApp.

**Why it helps:**
- Every no-show is a treatment slot worth several hundred pounds that gets lost. Reducing them even by a third recovers thousands of pounds a month.
- The team doesn't waste time setting up a room and preparing for someone who won't turn up.
- Patients who get the extra reminder genuinely appreciate it — it feels like care, not pestering.

---

### 9. Treatment Plan Drafting

After a new patient's assessment, the doctor clicks one button and receives a draft treatment plan — number of sessions, recommended treatment, expected timeline, and pricing — based on the patient's answers and photos.

**Why it helps:**
- A treatment plan that would normally take 15 minutes to write is ready to review and adjust in two.
- Plans are more consistent because every patient gets the same level of detail.
- Faster turnaround means patients receive their plan the same day they apply, while their interest is still high.

---

### 10. Smart Inbox Sorting

Every incoming message from a patient is automatically labelled — clinical question, booking enquiry, payment issue, complaint, urgent — so the right person sees it first.

**Why it helps:**
- Urgent messages get the team's attention immediately rather than sitting in a busy inbox.
- Routine enquiries (bookings, payments) can be handled by reception without taking up doctor time.
- Nothing falls through the cracks during a busy clinic day.

---

### 11. Revenue Opportunity Detector

The system quietly scans every patient on the books every day and flags people who are due for a follow-up but haven't booked one. The team sees a daily list of people to gently reach out to.

**Why it helps:**
- Recovers revenue from patients who would have otherwise drifted away.
- Suggests personalised re-engagement messages so the team isn't writing them from scratch.
- A typical clinic finds three to five thousand pounds of recoverable revenue every month using this kind of system.

---

### 12. Marketing Content on Demand

When the clinic wants to run a promotion or push a specific treatment, the team describes the goal in one sentence and receives ready-to-use Instagram captions, email subject lines, and WhatsApp broadcast messages in the clinic's tone of voice.

**Why it helps:**
- Marketing campaigns that would normally need a copywriter or agency are written in minutes.
- The clinic can be more active on social media without it eating into clinical time.
- Consistent brand voice across every channel.

---

## For Existing Patients — Keeping Them Engaged

### 13. AI Hair Care Coach

A friendly conversational assistant inside each patient's portal that they can chat with any time of day. It knows their treatment plan, their history, and the products they've been recommended.

**Why it helps:**
- Patients with late-night worries ("Can I wash my hair tonight?", "Is this shedding normal?") get an immediate, reassuring answer instead of waiting until morning.
- The team is only pulled in for the small percentage of questions that genuinely need a doctor — the routine ones are handled.
- Patients feel cared for around the clock without anyone having to be on call.

---

### 14. Progress Photo Coach

When a patient goes to upload their progress photo, the system shows them where they took the last one from and gives gentle live guidance ("tilt the phone slightly up", "move to better lighting") so every photo is taken from the same angle.

**Why it helps:**
- Consistent photos mean the doctor can actually compare progress meaningfully — and the patient can see their own improvement clearly.
- Patients are far more likely to keep uploading photos when the process feels easy and guided.
- Better photos mean better marketing material (with permission).

---

### 15. Personalised Progress Insights

Every month, the patient automatically receives a short, warm update on how their treatment is going — written by the system, based on their photos and check-ins.

**Why it helps:**
- Patients see steady evidence of progress, which keeps them motivated and engaged through the slower middle months of treatment.
- Drives repeat bookings — happy patients book more sessions and tell their friends.
- Reduces "is this working?" anxiety, which is the single biggest reason patients drop out.

---

### 16. Adaptive Aftercare

Instead of every patient receiving the same generic aftercare reminders, each patient receives messages that adapt to their treatment, their healing, and any concerns they've raised.

**Why it helps:**
- Aftercare feels personal — like a thoughtful concierge, not an automated system.
- Reduces the small worried calls that come in over the days following a treatment.
- Better recovery experiences lead to better reviews and word-of-mouth referrals.

---

## What We Would Build First

If we were starting tomorrow, the highest-impact features to launch first would be:

1. **Hair Loss Photo Analyser on the website** — the single biggest lead-generation tool we could add.
2. **24/7 AI Concierge** — captures every after-hours enquiry, books consultations automatically.
3. **Voice-to-Notes for the doctors** — saves close to an hour per doctor per working day, from day one.
4. **No-Show Prediction** — direct recovery of lost revenue, immediate financial return.
5. **AI Hair Care Coach in the patient portal** — keeps patients engaged and happy between treatments.

These five together would meaningfully change the day-to-day economics of the clinic — bringing in more leads, saving the team hours, and giving patients a noticeably better experience than they'd get anywhere else.

---

## Expected Impact

Brought together, the goal of this AI direction is simple:

- **More patients arriving** — through smarter lead capture on the website.
- **More patients converting** — through faster, more personalised follow-up.
- **More patients staying** — through better engagement between treatments.
- **Less time spent on admin** — so the team can focus on clinical work.
- **Less revenue lost** — to no-shows, drop-offs, and missed follow-up opportunities.

This isn't AI for the sake of being modern. Every feature listed here exists because it earns its place — by either bringing money in, keeping money from leaving, or giving the team back their time.
